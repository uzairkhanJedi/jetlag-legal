# Support

Jet Lag is made by one person. Email is the whole support channel, and it is
read by a human.

**[uzairkhan@gmail.com](mailto:uzairkhan@gmail.com)**

Please include your device and OS version, and the app version from the
**You** tab. Most reports are answered within a few days.

## Common questions

### Where has my plan gone?

Plans are generated from the trip, not stored separately — open the trip and
the plan is rebuilt. If a trip itself has disappeared, it was removed from
the device: everything is stored locally, so uninstalling the app or clearing
its data deletes it permanently. There is no cloud backup to restore from,
because nothing is ever uploaded.

### My reminders are not firing.

Reminders are scheduled locally by the app, so they depend on the
notification permission and on the OS not suspending the app. Check that
notifications are allowed for Jet Lag in your device settings. On Android,
battery optimisation can defer them; exempting Jet Lag fixes it.

### I paid, but the app still asks me to buy.

Open the **You** tab and tap **Restore Purchases**. That re-reads what your
App Store or Google Play account owns. The purchase is tied to that store
account, so make sure you are signed in with the same one you bought it on.

If it still does not unlock, email the address above with the store you
bought from and the date, and it will be sorted out.

### What does the flight lookup send?

The flight number and the date, and only when you tap **Look up**. Nothing
else goes with them. You can enter every flight by hand instead, and then the
app makes no network requests at all. The [privacy policy](../privacy/) sets
this out in full.

### Is this medical advice?

No. Jet Lag gives general wellness guidance about sleep, light, melatonin and
caffeine timing. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. If you
have a sleep disorder, are pregnant, or take medication, talk to a doctor.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or use your device's "clear app data" option on Android.
That is all of it — there is no server-side copy.
